Editing tips

Drag notes or ready-made groups from the palette. A highlighted note is a JOIN target; the empty measure is a NEW GROUP target. Clicking a measure does not add notes. Drop directly on a note to place the new note immediately AFTER it and connect their beams. Drop in empty measure space to start a NEW beam group. Purple cursor shows the insertion position. Double-click a note to toggle its accent. Use Delete to remove a selected note, Ctrl Z to undo, and Ctrl Y to redo.

🎸 Palm-Mute Practice Path

Work through quarter notes, eighth notes, and sixteenth notes. PM means gently rest the picking-hand palm near the bridge; OPEN means lift it to let the note ring. Each drill is a full 4/4 measure. Use the count-in and loop controls in Studio to repeat it.

Start slowly and aim for clean, even transitions.

Choose a drill to load its rhythm into the Studio. Your current measures will be replaced (Undo can restore them).
